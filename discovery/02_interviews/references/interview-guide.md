# Interview rules (Mom Test style)

- Ask about past behavior, not future intent
  ("when was the last time you…?" instead of "would you use…?").
- Don't pitch the product up front; understand the problem first.
- Quantify the pain: how much time or money it costs today, and how often.
- Identify who decides and who pays (not always the interviewee).
- Close by asking for commitment if there's a signal: next meeting, data, an intro, a pilot.
  A "how interesting" with no commitment is a negative signal.
- Compliments and advice aren't data. Facts and commitments are.
