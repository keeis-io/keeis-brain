  projects — matches the hypothesis's core pain (managers can't accompany every rep to every
  meeting).
- No stated visibility into what happens in the closing meetings he doesn't personally attend.

## Current cost / current alternative
- Selective in-person attendance, reserved for negotiations flagged as "difficult."
- Group-level closing-technique training, not individualized coaching from real conversation
  data.
- No tool or software mentioned as currently used for this.

## Commitment signals

## Verbatim quotes

- "Eso es muy variado, todos reciben la misma capacitación, pero en un promedio general ya con 6

## Contradictions / nuances vs. the hypothesis
- No outright contradiction. Broadly confirms the "can't be everywhere" pain and the "months to
  ramp up" claim (~6 months here).
- Partial nuance, not full confirmation: the hypothesis claims "nobody can correct [a new rep] on
  what they did wrong." Mauricio describes group/standardized training, not evidence of a
  specific individualized-feedback gap — this interview supports the *visibility* half of the
  hypothesis more directly than the *individualized correction* half. Treat the latter as still
  unconfirmed by this data point.
