# Validation criteria

- Minimum evidence: at least 5 interviews from the same segment before any verdict.
- Signals that count: they pay today for an alternative; they described the pain without
  prompting; there was concrete commitment (follow-up meeting, data, pilot, payment intent).
- Signals that DON'T count: "seems useful," "I'd use that," interviewee enthusiasm,
  opinions from friends or family.
- Biases to watch: confirmation (looking only for what validates), convenience sampling,
  leading questions.
- Honest refutation: if 0 of 5 pay today to solve the pain, the segment gets dropped or
  reframed. Dropping fast is a pipeline success, not a failure.
