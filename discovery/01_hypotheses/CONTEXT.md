# Stage 01 — Customer hypothesis

## Inputs
- Layer 3: `../../_config/current-strategy.md`
- Layer 3: `references/hypothesis-template.md`
- Layer 4: whatever the human contributes in conversation (candidate segment, contacts, context)

## Process
Write ONE falsifiable customer hypothesis following the template. No adjectives: specific
segment, observable pain, current alternative, estimated willingness to pay, riskiest
assumption, and a test with a numeric success criterion. Close with a list of 5–10 real,
reachable people or organizations to interview.

## Outputs
- `output/YYYY-MM-DD-hypothesis-<segment>.md`

## Review gate
Kendall and Isaac review and edit the hypothesis before scheduling interviews.
