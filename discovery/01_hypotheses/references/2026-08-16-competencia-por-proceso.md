# Competition by administrative process — Costa Rica

Supporting desk research for
`../output/2026-08-16-hypothesis-formalizacion-microempresas.md`. Captured 2026-08-16.

## Summary table

| Process | Who already does it in Costa Rica | Saturation |
|---|---|---|
| E-invoicing + compliance | **Alegra** (dominant — see detail below), Facture.cr, Facturele, e-FacturaCR, FactuPOS | High, but incomplete |
| POS / inventory | Alegra POS, PCPOS, Vendty, FLK POS, Logosoft, Square, Smart POS — several with free plans | Very high |
| Scheduling / appointments + WhatsApp | Well Beauty Software, **Caronte**, Checkfy, ReservaSimple (free up to 30 appointments/month), Agendelo, Yacita.ia (AI bot), Agendé | Very high |
| Customer service / WhatsApp CRM | DataCRM, Whato, Kommo, Magnetis Lab, RubikSoft, PorContar, Byte Intelligence | High |
| CCSS + municipal license + formalization combined in one flow | **Nobody identified** | Empty |

## Detail: Alegra (the strongest competitor)

Not just invoicing. Already integrates:
- Invoicing by voice, expense/invoice capture by photo or WhatsApp with AI
- Automatic bank reconciliation
- Collection reminders via WhatsApp (90.4% of businesses using it report a better collection
  rate)
- An MCP (Model Context Protocol) that lets users query their invoicing data from ChatGPT,
  Claude, or Perplexity
- 72,000+ registered SMEs in Costa Rica (64.5% women-led, 36% under 35)
- Plans starting at $9.99/month, no permanent free plan (only a 15-day trial)

**What Alegra doesn't solve**: assumes the user is already registered with Hacienda. Doesn't
calculate independent-worker CCSS contributions or the municipal license. Doesn't accompany the
step from informal to formal — it's designed for someone who already knows what an invoice is.

## Detail: Caronte (the "wide horizontal" already exists)

Caronte already combines appointment scheduling, group classes, WhatsApp automation, sales,
Costa Rican e-invoicing, inventory, staff, and reporting in a single product — aimed at salons,
clinics, workshops, and gyms. In practice, it's the wide horizontal product this research
originally contemplated. It doesn't solve CCSS or the municipal license either, and it isn't
built for someone who has never invoiced before.

## Conclusion

Costa Rica has far more digital competition for microenterprises than assumed at the start of
this analysis. In almost every operational process (scheduling, POS, customer service),
several mature players already exist, several with free plans and integrated AI. **The one job
that remains unowned, across every process reviewed, is taking someone from informal to
compliant with all three institutions (Hacienda, CCSS, Municipality) at once, without needing
an accountant.**
