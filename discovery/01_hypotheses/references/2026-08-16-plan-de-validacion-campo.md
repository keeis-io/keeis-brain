# Field validation plan — informal microenterprises in Costa Rica

Supporting desk research for
`../output/2026-08-16-hypothesis-formalizacion-microempresas.md`. Captured 2026-08-16. This is
the plan Stage 02 (`discovery/02_interviews`) executes once this hypothesis clears its review
gate — not yet run.

## Why this stage is mandatory

Everything in the linked reference docs is market research with hard data, but it stays a
hypothesis until confirmed with real microenterprise owners. No product gets decided or built
before completing this stage.

## Prioritized channel

In-person, face to face — not cold outreach. **Priority 1**: Feria del Agricultor and Cámara de
Comercio/gremios of Pérez Zeledón (geographic-proximity advantage). **Priority 2** (after
initial validation): partnerships with Banca para el Desarrollo or INAMU programs, which give
access to dozens of already-organized entrepreneurs. **Priority 3**: WhatsApp/Facebook groups
of entrepreneurs by canton, for awareness only, not cold selling.

## Key questions to validate

1. Is the #1 pain really the joint compliance burden (Hacienda + CCSS + patente), or is it
   something desk research didn't identify (collecting payment on time, getting customers,
   finding reliable employees)?
2. Of the three institutions, which generates the most real fear or lost time? (the pattern may
   not be even across all three)
3. What channel would they actually use — WhatsApp, an app, or would they prefer a human?
4. What does it cost them today, in money or lost time, not having this solved?
5. What do they pay, or would they pay, an accountant or gestor today to resolve it completely?
   (validates the premium pricing anchor)
6. Do they use SINPE Móvil to collect payment? What do they use today to track the business,
   even by hand?

## Week-by-week plan

| Week | Goal |
|---|---|
| 1 | 15-20 discovery interviews, no product shown |
| 2 | Analyze patterns, pick the #1 pain, build a simple clickable prototype of the "sign up + first receipt" flow |
| 3 | Show the prototype to 10 of the interviewees, ask price directly |
| 4 | Recruit 5-10 early adopters for a "Wizard of Oz" pilot (manual WhatsApp backend, no real product yet) |
| 5 | Measure real activation: did they complete their first receipt? Did they come back the following week? |
| 6 | Go/no-go decision |

## Decision criterion

If 3-5 of the early adopters are willing to pay recurringly and refer others, proceed to define
the product's real form with both partners and build the MVP. If not, switch channel or narrow
the niche (e.g. market vendors only, workshops only) before dropping the hypothesis entirely.
