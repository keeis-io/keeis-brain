# Pricing and viability — informal microenterprises in Costa Rica

Supporting desk research for
`../output/2026-08-16-hypothesis-formalizacion-microempresas.md`. Captured 2026-08-16.

> Numbers marked **estimated / unverified** require confirmation during field validation — they
> are not hard data yet.

## Pricing anchors

The right anchor isn't software competition (Alegra, $9.99/month) — that's the wrong
substitute. The target customer's real substitute is **hiring an accountant or gestor**, or
doing nothing and risking the fine.

- Official minimum rate for a Costa Rican CPA (Contador Público Autorizado): ₡22,695/professional
  hour.
- Estimating 2-3 hrs/month of basic bookkeeping (Hacienda + CCSS + patente), the formal floor
  would be ₡45,000–68,000/month (~$85-125). **Estimated**, not field data.
- Informal gestores (non-CPA) probably charge less than that formal floor, but there's no
  verified number — ask directly in interviews.

## Proposed tiered pricing

- **Free / freemium plan**: lowers entry friction and builds the daily-use habit. Necessary
  because this segment pays nothing today for business management.
- **Premium plan**: ₡15,000–25,000/month (~$28-46), anchored against the cost of an accountant,
  not against Alegra. Message: "for a fraction of what an accountant would cost, you resolve
  all three obligations at once."
- Additional revenue from a referral commission to an accountant when a case gets complicated.
- Institutional revenue (B2G/B2B): paid pilots via Banca para el Desarrollo, INAMU, or
  municipalities as part of formalization programs — potentially $500-1,500/month per contract,
  equivalent to 100-300 individual users.

## Why the high price can't be assumed from day one

1. **Value perception isn't automatic**: a market vendor doesn't arrive thinking about how much
   an accountant would save them — they arrive afraid of Hacienda. The product has to
   communicate that saving explicitly.
2. **Willingness to pay ≠ ability to pay**: even understanding the value, they may not have the
   cash flow available that month.

The tiered strategy (free entry + premium anchored to the gestor) reconciles both: low adoption
friction while still leaving real room to charge more once trust is earned.

## Year-1 viability numbers (estimated)

| Variable | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Acquisition pace with no ad budget, in-person channel | 10-25 new users/month |
| Active users by end of year 1 | 150-350 |
| Estimated variable cost per user (WhatsApp Business API + e-signature) | $3-6/user/month — **unverified, quote with a provider** |
| Estimated net margin per user (~$9/month plan) | $3-5/month |
| Paying users needed for real combined profit (~₡400,000-600,000/month between two partners) | 350-500, over 18-30 months |

With the premium plan anchored to the cost of a gestor (₡15,000-25,000/month), margin per user
rises substantially and the number of users needed for real profitability drops proportionally
— but that price and that willingness to pay must be confirmed in the field before assuming
them.

## Critical pending item before committing to these numbers

Get a direct quote from a Costa Rican e-invoicing provider for the real cost per receipt and
per WhatsApp Business API integration. It's as important as the interviews with business
owners.
