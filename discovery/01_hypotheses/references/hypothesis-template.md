# Customer hypothesis template

- **Segment**: who exactly ("companies" isn't a segment; "private parking lot managers in the GAM" is).
- **Pain**: what costs them time or money today, observably.
- **Current alternative**: what they do today to solve it (includes "nothing" and "a person with a spreadsheet").
- **Why they'd pay**: concrete value, not "efficiency."
- **Reference price**: what they pay today for the alternative.
- **Riskiest assumption**: the thing that, if false, kills the whole hypothesis.
- **Test**: what evidence confirms or refutes it, with a numeric criterion
  (e.g. "at least 3 of 8 interviewees describe the pain without us suggesting it,
  AND they pay today to solve it somehow").
- **Skin-in-the-game evidence** (Savoia, *The Right It*): committed time, money,
  shared data, or a letter of intent. Polite opinions don't count as data.
- **People to contact**: real name, organization, contact channel.
