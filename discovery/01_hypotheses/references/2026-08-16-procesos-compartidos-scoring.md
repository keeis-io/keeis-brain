# Shared processes across microenterprises — prioritization

Supporting desk research for
`../output/2026-08-16-hypothesis-formalizacion-microempresas.md`. Captured 2026-08-16.

## Methodology

Each process was scored 1-5 on five criteria: horizontal applicability (how many
microenterprises it touches regardless of industry), current pain, obligatoriness (concrete
legal consequence), frequency of use, and competitive gap specific to the micro/informal
segment.

## Ranking (out of 25 points)

| Process | Horizontal | Pain | Obligatoriness | Frequency | Gap | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hacienda (invoicing + declaration) | 5 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | **23** |
| Cash-flow control / separating personal-business money | 5 | 5 | 1 | 5 | 5 | **21** |
| CCSS (independent/employer) | 5 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | **20** |
| Collecting and reconciling sales (SINPE + cash) | 5 | 4 | 2 | 5 | 3 | **19** |
| Municipal business license (patente) | 5 | 3 | 4 | 2 | 5 | **19** |
| Sanitary operating permit | 3 | 4 | 4 | 1 | 4 | 16 |
| WhatsApp customer service | 5 | 3 | 1 | 5 | 1 | 15 |
| Staff/payroll management | 2 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 13 |
| Inventory/supplies | 3 | 3 | 1 | 3 | 2 | 12 |
| INS risk policy | 2 | 2 | 3 | 1 | 3 | 11 |
| Scheduling/appointments | 3 | 2 | 1 | 4 | 1 | 11 |

## The key finding

The top five scored processes aren't five separate products — they're **the same piece of
information seen from five angles**. Every sale collected (via SINPE or cash) is the base data
point that feeds:

```
Sale record (SINPE + cash)
        │
        ├──► E-invoicing (to Hacienda)
        ├──► CCSS contribution calculation
        ├──► Basis for the municipal license
        └──► Real cash flow (separated from personal money)
```

WhatsApp/customer service, inventory, and scheduling ranked lower on purpose: they don't share
this same data root, each would require separate development, and they're processes where
mature competition already exists (see `2026-08-16-competencia-por-proceso.md`). They're valid
candidates for future expansion, not for the entry point.
