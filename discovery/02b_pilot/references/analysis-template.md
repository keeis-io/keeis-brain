# Analysis template — visit sheet and readout

The fixed template every recorded conversation goes through, so Kendall's and Isaac's output is
comparable across visits, pilots, and profiles (priority #3 in
`../output/2026-09-05-pilot-deliverable-design.md`). The customer-facing shape of the sheet is
fixed by §3.1 and Annex A of `../output/2026-09-05-pilot-proposal-consultative-sales.md` — do not
drift from it per pilot; if a change is needed, change it here, dated, and re-issue.

## Working rules

- Transcribe with a locally-run open-source model on our own laptops. No audio to any third-party
  service (decision 2026-09-05). Read the transcript **with** the audio at least once; ASR
  quality on Costa Rican Spanish is the floor to check (the Purdy WhatsApp auto-transcript was
  unusable without reconstruction).
- Skip personal or non-business segments entirely: not transcribed, not summarized, not quoted.
- People appear by role, never by name. Everything identifying is deleted the day the readout is
  delivered; what we keep afterwards is anonymized conclusions and minutes per stage only.
- Every claim on the sheet points to a moment in the recording (timestamp in our working copy,
  never on the delivered sheet).
- Log hours per stage in the pilot run file as you go, not from memory afterwards.

## Conversation / visit sheet (delivered, one page, Spanish, this order)

```
HOJA DE CONVERSACIÓN · #NN · [lugar / tipo de cliente] · Vendedor: [nombre] · [día, hora, duración] ·
Cómo llegó / solicitud original: [según el cliente]

1. Qué hacer ahora            — 2–3 acciones concretas, con verbo, que salen de la conversación
2. Qué pidió exactamente      — requerimiento en palabras del cliente; condiciones (plazo,
                                instalación, capacitación, mantenimiento, pago, voltaje…)
3. Quién decide               — presentes (rol) · mencionados no presentes (rol) · lectura de
                                quién define la especificación y quién cierra la compra
4. Mencionado y no cotizado   — necesidades adyacentes que hoy no llegarían a la oferta
5. Objeciones y estado        — objeción · respuesta del vendedor · resuelta / parcial / abierta

Copia para el vendedor        — misma información + "lo que funcionó" (1) + "una cosa para
                                probar" (1). Sin calificación, sin comparación con nadie.
```

## Internal analysis notes (not delivered; deleted with the material)

Per visit, for the readout and the cross-pilot comparison:

- Information that would have been lost with paper notes (list; the basis of the readout's first
  point).
- Opportunities surfaced vs. opportunities in the original request (count both).
- Decision structure observed (single decider / specifier + approver / committee).
- Objection types and resolution rate.
- Anything the fixed template could not hold — the signal that the template needs a change or
  that this profile differs from the last (assumption #7).
- Time: transfer → transcription → reading → analysis → writing → delivery, in minutes.

## Readout (20 min, live, this order)

1. What is being lost between visit and quote — with two or three examples from the sheets.
2. Opportunities that surfaced, and how many reached an offer (ask; do not assume).
3. Patterns in how clients decide and object across the visits.
4. What we would do differently if this continued.
5. **The three observable questions** (proposal §9): a quote built from a sheet? an unquoted
   opportunity that reached an offer? an action the manager took?
6. **The continuation offer**, stated plainly with its terms, then silence. Record the answer as
   exactly one of: paid / asked to continue unprompted / neither.

## Rep conversation (10 min, after the readout)

- "¿Saber que se grababa cambió cómo hablaste con el cliente? ¿En qué?" (assumption #3)
- "¿Cómo se sintió hacer la pregunta de permiso? ¿Alguien dudó o dijo que no? ¿Qué pasó después?" (#8)
- "¿La hoja te sirvió para algo concreto? ¿Para qué?" (#1, #4)
- "¿Sentiste esto como supervisión o como ayuda?" — ask it exactly this openly (#9)
