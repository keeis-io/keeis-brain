## 1. What the company gets

For **one week**, with no cost and no commitment: every field or closing conversation their reps
record gets analyzed by hand, and the manager receives a structured read of what happened in
conversations they were not in.

Concretely, per visit, on one page:
- **Who actually decides** — who was in the room, who was mentioned but absent, who appears to hold
  the real veto.
- **What the client actually asked for** — the technical requirement as stated, captured intact
  rather than reconstructed from memory back at the office.
- **What was mentioned and never quoted** — adjacent needs that surfaced in conversation and did
  not make it into the offer.
- **Objections raised, and whether they were resolved** — with what was actually said, not a typed
  summary.

Delivered as a **15-minute live readout** at the end of the week, not a file dropped in an inbox.
The manager listens to no recordings and reviews no transcripts. That is the whole point.

## 2. What we ask of them

Stated plainly, because a free pilot with hidden costs is not free:
- Reps record their conversations for one week. **[DECISION PENDING: on what device / with what tool.]**
- Consent from the end client, obtained before recording. **[DECISION PENDING: mechanism — see §5.]**
- ~15 minutes of the manager's time for the closing readout.
- One short conversation with a participating rep afterwards, about how being recorded felt.

That last item is not filler — it is how we find out whether the recording itself damaged the
conversation, which is one of the two things that could sink this entire approach.

## 3. What we get, said out loud

The plan forbids framing this as a favor or as validation of our own idea. The honest framing is a
two-way exchange, and it is stronger than pretending we get nothing:

- We learn whether the analysis produces anything a manager actually acts on.
- We learn how long it takes us to produce it — the first real measurement of what this costs to
  deliver.
- We learn how to run the process itself: offering it, executing it, delivering it.

Said to the company in one line: *"ustedes obtienen los hallazgos; nosotros aprendemos si esto
sirve de verdad y cuánto cuesta hacerlo bien."* Nothing about that weakens the offer — it is the
reason there is no price attached.

## 4. What we explicitly do NOT do

Named up front, because every one of these is a fear a sales manager will have and because two of
them came directly out of the interviews:

- **No ranking of reps against each other.** Not in the first pilot, not in the readout.
- **No compliance or follow-up reporting.** We do not tell the manager who failed to follow up.
  That is a different product, and deliberately not this one.
- **Nothing goes to HR, ownership, or anyone the manager did not designate.**
- **No recording is shared outside the two of us.**
- **No automation, no AI decisions.** Two people listen and write. If that changes later, it
  changes with their knowledge.
- **They can stop at any point** and the material is destroyed on request.

## 5. The three decisions blocking this

Each of these is a sentence in the pitch. They cannot be left vague — a manager who cannot get a
straight answer here will decline, correctly.

| # | Question | Why it blocks the offer | Plan reference |

## 6. Draft pitch — Spanish, for the first contact

Fill the three bracketed blanks before sending. Register per `_config/voice.md`: this is
external-facing, so polished rather than internal-terse.

```
Buenas [nombre],

Le escribo con algo concreto, sin costo y sin compromiso.

Trabajamos con equipos de venta consultiva donde el gerente no puede estar en cada
visita — y donde, aunque el CRM registre que hubo contacto, no queda nada de lo que
realmente se conversó con el cliente.

La propuesta es simple: durante una semana, sus vendedores graban sus conversaciones
de campo. Nosotros las analizamos a mano, una por una, y al final de la semana nos
sentamos 15 minutos con usted a entregarle lo que encontramos.

De cada visita le devolvemos, en una página:
· quién decide realmente en ese negocio — incluyendo a quien se mencionó pero no
  estaba en la reunión
· qué pidió exactamente el cliente, en sus palabras, sin depender de lo que el
  vendedor logró anotar
· qué se mencionó en la conversación y nunca llegó a la oferta
· qué objeciones salieron y si quedaron resueltas

Usted no escucha ninguna grabación ni lee ninguna transcripción. Ese es el punto.

Lo que necesitamos de su lado: que sus vendedores graben durante esa semana, el
consentimiento del cliente antes de grabar, sus 15 minutos al final, y una
conversación corta con uno de los vendedores sobre cómo se sintió el ejercicio.

Sobre el manejo de la información: las grabaciones se almacenan en [HERRAMIENTA, CON
ACCESO RESTRINGIDO A LAS DOS PERSONAS QUE ANALIZAN] y se eliminan [VENTANA DE
RETENCIÓN] después de la entrega. Al cliente final se le pide consentimiento
[MECANISMO], antes de grabar. Si en cualquier momento quiere detenerlo, se detiene y
se destruye el material.

Y para ser claros sobre lo que NO hacemos: no rankeamos vendedores entre sí, no le
reportamos a nadie quién dio o no dio seguimiento, y nada sale de nosotros dos.

Somos honestos sobre por qué es gratis: ustedes obtienen los hallazgos, y nosotros
aprendemos si esto sirve de verdad y cuánto cuesta hacerlo bien. Es un intercambio,
no un favor.

¿Le parece si conversamos 15 minutos esta semana o la próxima?

[firma]
```

## 7. What this does not settle

- **Price.** Nothing here mentions money, correctly — the pilot is free by design. Willingness to
  pay gets tested at Phase 5, after delivery, per the plan's threshold: payment or an unprompted
  request to continue. Not before.
- **Which company receives it first.** Route B recruiting criteria are in the lean canvas
  (`2026-09-05-lean-canvas-consultative-sales.md`, block 2): consultative high-ticket sales,
  roughly **≥8 reps** — below that the manager can still be in the room and reports no pain.
