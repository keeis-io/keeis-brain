## How we will know the pilot validated something

The criterion is already defined and does not move (plan Phase 5; hypothesis `Test` field;
`../../03_synthesis/CONTEXT.md`):

- **Threshold**: at least **3 completed pilots** (the roadmap's definition — attempted pilots do
  not count) across at least **2 distinct sales profiles**.
- **What counts, per pilot**: the company **pays** something to continue (any amount), or
  **asks to continue without being prompted** — recorded at the readout as one of exactly three
  values in the run file.
- **What does not count**: "esto tiene valor," "muy interesante," a compliment, a referral
  offered out of politeness, or continued use that we suggested.
- **Governing hypothesis** (experiments file): at least 2 of the first 3 completed pilots produce
  a counting signal. Fewer than that, and the verdict at Block 5 is "refuted" or "insufficient
  evidence," with the failing claim named (pain not real / form wrong / consent unworkable).
- **Alongside the verdict**, three numbers the pilots must have produced or Phase 5 cannot
  decide the SaaS-vs-consultancy fork: measured hours per pilot, consent acceptance rate, and
  the observable-use answers (quote from a sheet / opportunity to offer / manager action).
