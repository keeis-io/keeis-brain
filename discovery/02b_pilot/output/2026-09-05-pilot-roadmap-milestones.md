# Pilot roadmap — two-week blocks, from first confirmed company to the Phase 5 decision — 2026-09-05

Execution roadmap for Phases 3–5 of
`../../01_hypotheses/references/2026-09-04-plan-de-validacion-campo-ventas-consultivas.md`.
Companion to `2026-09-05-pilot-proposal-consultative-sales.md` (what a company is offered) and
`2026-09-05-pilot-next-steps.md` (what to do this week). Each block states what runs, what must
be closed at its end, and what happens if it is not.

## Two rules that govern every block

1. **Block 1 is the only block with a duration we can state.** The plan and the old pilot offer
   said "one week per pilot." Assumption #6 in `2026-09-04-assumptions-map.md` says the real
   analysis time has never been measured, and it is what the first pilot measures. So Block 1 is
   sized around **one** pilot with margin (a 5-day recording window, a 2-business-day turnaround
   per visit sheet, a readout within 5 business days of the last recording, and the extension
   clause in the proposal's §4). Blocks 2–5 carry a *nominal* two-week duration that is
   **recalibrated with Block 1's measured hours**, not with a prior estimate. The recalibration is
   itself a closing item of Block 2.
2. **Pilots run one at a time until Block 1 says otherwise.** Two people with coursework
   (assumption #14) do not run three concurrent manual pilots on an assumption. Parallelism is
   allowed only if Block 1's measured load leaves room for it.

**Clock start (T0):** the day the first company confirms a start date. Not the day the proposal
is sent. Everything before T0 is in `2026-09-05-pilot-next-steps.md`, not here.

**"Completed pilot"** (the unit Phase 5 counts): kickoff held, ≥5 conversations recorded with
consent, every visit sheet delivered, readout delivered, the commitment question asked with the
continuation offer on the table, the rep conversation held, and hours per stage logged in the
pilot's run file. A pilot missing any of these is *attempted*, not completed.

**Illustrative calendar only:** if T0 lands the week of 2026-09-14, the nominal plan puts the
Phase 5 decision point around the second half of November 2026. That date moves with Block 1's
measurements and with recruiting; it is not a commitment.

## Block 1 — Weeks 1–2 from T0 — Pilot 1 (first profile: hybrid — agency digital leads, close at a dealership)

**Runs**
- Before Day 0: the authority question settled — if the reps belong to the dealership, its sales
  manager has approved and is at the kickoff. No kickoff without that.
- Day 0: kickoff (20 min, sales manager + participating reps): conversation eligibility agreed,
  the consent question rehearsed once, recorder test on each rep's phone, upload folder access.
- Days 1–5: recording week. Visit sheets delivered within 2 business days of each upload, to rep
  and manager. Analysis with the fixed template in `../references/analysis-template.md`.
- End of the recording week: the dealership flags, per recorded conversation, advanced / closed /
  neither (Roberto Solano's conversion-pattern design — testable here because the cycle is short).
- Days 6–10: remaining sheets; readout (20 min) within 5 business days of the last recording,
  ending with the three observable questions (proposal §9) and the continuation offer. **All
  material deleted the same day, deletion confirmed by email.**
- Rep conversation (10 min) after the readout: "did knowing it was recorded change how you
  spoke?" (assumptions #3 and #9).
- Throughout: log hours per stage (transfer, transcription, analysis, writing, delivery) and
  every process friction in `output/YYYY-MM-DD-pilot-<company>.md` per
  `../references/pilot-run-template.md`.

**Must be closed by the end of the block**
- Pilot 1 completed, per the definition above.
- Commitment result recorded as one of exactly three values: **paid** (any amount), **asked to
  continue unprompted**, or **neither**. Nothing else counts (plan Phase 5).
- Hours per stage measured. This is the number every later block is re-sized from.
- Consent outcomes counted: asked / accepted / declined / withdrawn.
- The three observable-use answers recorded (quote built from a sheet; unquoted opportunity
  reached an offer; manager action taken).
- Human gate: Kendall and Isaac review the run file before recruiting anyone else
  (`../CONTEXT.md`, review gate).

**If not closed**
- **Reschedule (once, +1 block)** when the cause is on the company's side and is about volume or
  calendar: fewer than 5 recorded visits in the window, a manager who could not make the readout.
  The extension clause in the proposal already covers the first of these.
- **Abandon this company, keep the route** when consent fails systematically (clients decline
  most of the time), or reps stop recording after a restart. Both are findings, not delays: log
  them and move to the next candidate in Block 2. A consent failure rate that high also reopens
  assumption #8 and triggers a legal check before any other company is approached.
- **Never** extend Block 1 past two extensions. If a pilot cannot complete in six weeks, the
  process, not the company, is the problem: stop and redesign before recruiting again.

## Block 2 — Weeks 3–4 — Recalibrate, review, recruit the second profile

**Runs**
- Recalibrate: from Block 1's measured hours, set the real duration and capacity of a pilot
  (visits per week the two of us can turn around in 2 business days). Rewrite the nominal
  durations of Blocks 3–5 in this file, dated.
- Review gate on Pilot 1: what changes in the proposal, the template, the consent script, the
  readout. Apply the changes before the next company sees any of it.
- Recruit Pilot 2 in a **second sales profile** (Phase 5 requires two). First candidate:
  Route C — Saylen Morales / ENHMED (technical B2B distribution, no field CRM), with the proposal
  variant already prepared during Block 1 (cover, §2 and sample sheet rewritten for field visits
  and requirements capture; open tenders excludable). Then Route B — own search. Disrupt (real
  estate, incumbent CRM) is a harder but in-segment fallback. Purdy is not a candidate: the pain
  is not felt there and the ask was for compliance control, which is out of scope by decision.
- Second-profile value-proposition pass (decision of 2026-09-05: each profile gets its own),
  and the proposal's cover and §2 rewritten from that company's own words.
- If Pilot 1's company asked to continue or paid: define and start the continuation on agreed
  terms. This runs alongside recruiting, and its hours are logged separately.

**Must be closed by the end of the block**
- Durations of Blocks 3–5 rewritten from data.
- Pilot 2 company confirmed with a start date, in a profile distinct from Pilot 1's.
- Proposal v2 (with Block 1's corrections) ready.

**If not closed**
- **Reschedule (+1 block)** if a candidate is in conversation but not yet confirmed.
- **Escalate, don't drift** if by the end of Block 3 no second company has confirmed: the pilot
  channel has a recruiting problem, which is a different problem from the product. Decide
  explicitly between widening Route B, going back to an already-interviewed contact with the
  sample sheet in hand (experiment 1 in `2026-09-05-experiments-brainstorm.md`), or logging the
  channel as failing. Do not fill the gap with a third pilot in the *same* profile as Pilot 1 —
  that does not advance Phase 5's threshold.

## Block 3 — Weeks 5–6 (nominal, re-sized in Block 2) — Pilot 2; recruit Pilot 3

**Runs**
- Pilot 2 executed end to end, same instrumentation as Pilot 1. In a long-cycle profile (ENHMED,
  tender-driven) the conversion flag is not observable within the window — the readout leans on
  the sheets' content (requirements captured, opportunities surfaced) rather than on
  converted-vs-not patterns. Note the difference explicitly: it is itself evidence for #7.
- Recruit Pilot 3: either a second company in Pilot 1's profile (to see whether the finding
  repeats) or a third profile. Priority per the plan's Phase 4: profiles with confirmed signal
  first (hybrid high-ticket with few reps; technical consultative B2B).

**Must be closed by the end of the block**
- Pilot 2 completed, with its commitment result and hours logged.
- Pilot 3 company confirmed with a start date.
- First cross-profile comparison written (assumption #7: does the analysis generalize?). Even
  two pilots are enough to start the comparison; it is refined in Block 5.

**If not closed**
- **Reschedule (+1 block)** for calendar reasons, once.
- **Abandon the company, keep the route** on the same triggers as Block 1.
- If Pilot 2 completes but Pilot 3 is unconfirmed: continue recruiting into Block 4, and use the
  gap to run experiments 1 and 3 from the experiments file, which need no company.

## Block 4 — Weeks 7–8 (nominal) — Pilot 3

**Runs**
- Pilot 3 executed end to end.
- Pre-synthesis: pull the three run files, the consent counts, the hours, and the three
  commitment results into one table.
- Prepare the Phase 5 inputs: the SaaS-vs-consultancy fork (Roberto Solano's, deferred by the
  plan to exactly this point) gets a one-page position from each of Kendall and Isaac, written
  independently, before the decision block.

**Must be closed by the end of the block**
- Pilot 3 completed. Three completed pilots across at least two profiles, or an explicit note of
  what is missing and why.

**If not closed**
- **Reschedule (+1 block)**, once, for calendar reasons.
- If, at the end of the extension, only two pilots are complete: **do not issue a verdict**.
  Phase 5's threshold is a floor, not a target. Record "insufficient evidence" with what is
  missing and go back to recruiting. Roberto's warning applies: two examples can show "no
  correlation" by chance.

## Block 5 — Weeks 9–10 (nominal) — Phase 5 decision point

**Runs**
- Synthesis run in `../../03_synthesis/output/` per that stage's `CONTEXT.md`: evidence weighed
  against the riskiest assumption; verdict **validated / refuted / insufficient evidence**;
  proposed update to `_config/current-strategy.md`, not applied by the agent.
- The commitment tally is the verdict's spine: how many of the three companies **paid or asked
  to continue unprompted**. "This has value" after a free pilot counts for nothing, per the plan.
- Resolve the strategic fork: build a generic SaaS now, or continue as an adaptable consultancy
  and productize later. By this point there are three engagements' worth of evidence about how
  the consultancy option feels to operate (assumption #13), plus the measured cost per pilot
  (assumption #6), which is what makes the fork a real decision rather than a preference.
- Decide what Phase 6 (product development) is, if and only if the verdict is "validated."

**Must be closed by the end of the block**
- A written verdict in `03_synthesis/output/`.
- A decision-log entry in `_config/decisions.md` with the fork resolved, dated, with reasons.
- If validated: the scope of Phase 6, and whether any of the three companies becomes a paying
  first client on continuation terms.
- If refuted or insufficient: which claim failed (pain not real / form wrong / consent made it
  unworkable — the three suspects named in `2026-09-04-assumptions-prioritized.md`), and whether
  the segment is dropped, reframed, or re-piloted with a different form.

**If not closed**
- There is no reschedule for a decision. If the evidence is not enough, the verdict is
  "insufficient evidence" and the roadmap loops back to Block 3 with a named gap. Dropping fast
  is a pipeline success, not a failure (`../../03_synthesis/references/validation-criteria.md`).

---

## What this roadmap deliberately does not contain

- **Pricing.** The continuation offer's terms are decided before the first readout (a next-step
  item), not here, and willingness to pay is only ever tested after delivery.
- **Parallel pilots.** Allowed only after Block 1's load measurement, and only if the measured
  turnaround stays within 2 business days per sheet with two pilots running.
- **Any automation.** Phase 6 material, gated on Phase 5.
