# Stage 02b — Field pilot (concierge MVP)

Parallel evidence channel to `02_interviews`, not a successor to it. Cold-email outreach stopped
converting; as of 2026-09-04 this stage gathers evidence by running a manual pilot — recording
real vendor–client conversations, analyzing them by hand, and handing findings back to the
manager — per
`../01_hypotheses/references/2026-09-04-plan-de-validacion-campo-ventas-consultivas.md`.
Everything produced here feeds `03_synthesis`.

Why manual: the pilot validates product AND process at once before anything gets built. A finding
about how the team offers, executes, and delivers counts as much as a finding about the product.

- Layer 4: `../01_hypotheses/output/2026-08-26-hypothesis-consultative-sales-teams.md` — active hypothesis

## Process
Two phases with a human gate between them.

**(a) Preparation** — sharpen the offer before recruiting anyone: value proposition, assumption
map, and the prioritized list of what the pilot actually has to test. Two blockers must be closed
here before any company is contacted:
1. the consent mechanism for the **end client** (a third party who never opted into the pilot), and
2. which product form the pilot tests — evidence points at three competing forms, and running a
   generic pilot makes a negative result uninterpretable.

**(b) Execution** — one pilot at a time: record, analyze manually, deliver findings, ask for
commitment. Log process friction alongside product findings. Per the plan's Phase 5, "this has
value" after a free pilot does not count; payment (even symbolic) or an unprompted request to
continue does.

## Review gate
Nothing is offered to a company until Kendall and Isaac sign off on both the proposal and the
consent mechanism. After each pilot: review before recruiting the next.
