Startup Stages and Funding Path
Don't plan a three-year roadmap: focus on milestone-to-milestone progression

Pre-seed: validate idea, explore with potential customers, assess target market (roughly 3 months)

Seed: build product, show customer traction, cover one year of operations

Series A: full venture round, requires defensible market position

Investors want to see traction, not just technology

Early conversations with potential customers

Letters of intent or small paid proof-of-concept contracts ($2-5K) as evidence of demand

Technical risk is no longer the bar; market traction is

Pay yourself a salary out of investment: no need to choose between startup and income if funding is secured
