# Stage 02 — Notes

## Inputs
- Layer 4: raw notes or transcript the human leaves in `output/raw/`
- Layer 4: `../01_preparation/output/<matching prep>`

## Process
Structure: agreements, disagreements, new data, our commitments, the other party's
commitments, and signals worth calibrating (expressed interest ≠ commitment). Don't
invent anything; verbatim quotes when they matter.

## Outputs
- `output/YYYY-MM-DD-notes-<who>.md`

## Review gate
The human corrects it while the meeting is still fresh.
