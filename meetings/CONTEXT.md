# Pipeline: Meetings

One run = one meeting (with an investor, between partners, with a third party). Market
validation conversations do NOT go here: they go in `discovery/02_interviews`.
Stages: `01_preparation` → `02_notes` → `03_actions`.
Output to the world: from approved actions, **derive** whatever documents apply into their
sector, and **activate** anything external (no destination configured today) — see the `derive`
and `activate` skills.
If the meeting belongs to a project, the run declares `project: <slug>` on the first line.
