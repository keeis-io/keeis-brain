# Review checklist

- Does every number have a source or an explicit assumption?
- Is the market calculated bottom-up?
- Does the traction section report only facts (nothing aspirational dressed up as an achievement)?
- If there's an ask, is it specific: amount, use, milestones?
- Are the remaining `[PENDING]` items listed up front with a plan to close them?
- Does the document survive the question "who pays for this today, and how much?"
- Length: 12 pages or fewer.
