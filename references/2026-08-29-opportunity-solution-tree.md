# Opportunity Solution Tree (OST)

Source: [phuryn/pm-skills](https://github.com/phuryn/pm-skills), `pm-product-discovery/skills/opportunity-solution-tree`
(MIT License, © Paweł Huryn / The Product Compass). Adapted for reference use.

## When to use

To map customer opportunities and candidate solutions before committing to one hypothesis —
so "the active hypothesis" is the best-scored one, not just the first one considered.
Manual use — not wired into any pipeline stage; update by hand when revisiting discovery direction.

## Origin

Teresa Torres, *Continuous Discovery Habits*. Prevents premature solution commitment by
requiring the opportunity landscape to be mapped before picking a solution.

## Four-level structure

1. **Desired outcome** — one measurable business/product metric (e.g. "validate a segment
   with real willingness to pay").
2. **Opportunities** — customer needs/pains found in research, framed from the customer's
   side: "I struggle to...", "I wish I could...". Prioritize by Opportunity Score =
   Importance × (1 − Satisfaction).
3. **Solutions** — multiple possible approaches per opportunity. Generate at least 3 before
   settling on one.
4. **Experiments** — fast, low-cost tests validating a solution against the opportunity
   (Value, Usability, Viability, Feasibility assumptions).

## Key principles

- Focus on one outcome at a time.
- Prioritize opportunities (problems), not features.
- Generate 3+ solutions per opportunity before choosing.
- Loop back and iterate when an experiment fails.
- Update the tree periodically as new evidence comes in — it's a living map, not a
  one-time document.

## Process

1. Define a single, measurable desired outcome.
2. List 3–7 opportunities from customer research (interview notes, synthesis).
3. Rank opportunities by Opportunity Score.
4. Brainstorm 3+ solutions per prioritized opportunity.
5. Design fast experiments with clear hypotheses and numeric success metrics.
6. Visualize the tree hierarchically (outcome → opportunities → solutions → experiments).
