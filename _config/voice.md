# Voice and writing

## Internal
Direct, no dressing up. Falsifiable claims. Numbers with a source or flagged `[PENDING]`.

## External — investors (e.g. Erik)
Professional and sober. Evidence before vision. Risks are named up front:
it builds more trust than hiding them, especially with someone quantitatively trained.

## Forbidden in any material
"Revolutionary," vanity metrics, projections without explicit assumptions,
and attributing commitments or interest that don't exist in writing.
