# Keeis brain — HOME

This is the entry point for browsing this repo in Obsidian's graph — a navigation layer, not a
source of truth. The repo itself, read through `CLAUDE.md` and each `CONTEXT.md`, remains
authoritative; this file and the `MOC.md` hubs only exist to make folders visible as branches
in the graph.

## Branches

- [[discovery/MOC|Discovery]] — market validation: hypotheses, interviews, field pilot, synthesis.
- [[meetings/MOC|Meetings]] — preparation, notes, and actions for every meeting.
- [[business-plan/MOC|Business plan]] — the investor-reviewable plan, research through review.
- [[projects/MOC|Projects]] — Keeis (the company) and `plates`, with their specs/architecture/canvas.

## Standing reference

- [[current-strategy|Current strategy]] — what the active focus is (max 2 fronts) and why.
- [[decisions|Decision log]] — why past strategy and architecture calls were made, dated and reasoned.
- [[company|Company]] — who Keeis is, its founders, and its current status.
- [[pending|Open commitments]] — what is owed and by whom, across every meeting.

## Where things go

- [[ideas/README|Ideas]] — raw ideas land here first, capture only; reviewed weekly and moved out.
- [[references/README|References]] — external material (benchmarking, market research) with no clear pipeline yet, capture only.
